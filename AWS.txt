AWS
1.Log into the using the link https://awsacademy.instructure.com/login/canvas
2.under the modules tab, launch the learner's lab
3.click on the start lab and click on the green button.
4.Search or Ec2 and click on launch instace.
5.Enter the name of the instance
6. Select ubuntu  in quickstart menu.
7.In the amazon machine image, select either ubuntu 26.04 or 24.04 of the instance
8.In the instance type, choose the one with free tier eligible option(t3.micro)
9. Under the security , click on the create new key-pair, give a name to the key and save it
10. Ensure under the network, all the checkboxes are ticked.
11. click on the launch instance , near the bottom right.
12. Navigate to the instances near the top right of the window.
13.Click on instance, under the security tab, click onsecurity groups, click on edit inbound rules.
14.Click on add rule custom tcp, port=8080, ip=0.0.0.0/0, description=optional/tomcat. Click on save rules
15. Come back to instances, select the created instance, in the new tab , select the browser to connect directly, or use the ssh client and run the 4th instruction.
16.After connecting to the instance, follow the commands
